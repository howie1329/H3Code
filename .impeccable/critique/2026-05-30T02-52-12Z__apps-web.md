## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Reads as **moderately AI-generated**. The copy is more specific than typical slop (no "supercharge" line), but the **layout grammar is textbook 2024 dev-tool landing**: uppercase kickers on every section, a three-column "features" band, numbered `01 / 02 / 03` workflow, and badge chips under the hero. There is **no product screenshot or visual proof** of the desktop workbench, which makes the page feel like a placeholder relative to your own `apps/web/DESIGN.md` brief (screenshot-framed sections on a dark canvas).

## Overall Impression

The page is **clear and calm** but **under-built for a brand surface**. It explains H3 Code in prose where the design system already calls for **showing the app**. The single biggest opportunity: **replace the generic section scaffold with one strong product frame** (desktop screenshot in `bg-card`) and cut the repeated eyebrows and numbered workflow.

## What's Working

1. **Hero message is concrete.** "Run AI coding agents across local repositories" states the job without buzzword stacking.
2. **Low cognitive load.** One primary column, few choices, readable hierarchy in the hero.
3. **Token discipline in code.** Colors flow through `app.css` and semantic utilities; dark mode is implemented and looks closer to the intended Linear-like canvas than light mode.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Spec drift: marketing DESIGN.md is not reflected on the page

- **What:** `apps/web/DESIGN.md` promises dark-canvas marketing, display type with measured negative tracking, and **product UI screenshots in `bg-card` panels**. The live page is a text-only scroll with no screenshot component and light tokens as `:root` default.
- **Why it matters:** Evaluators cannot see the product; agents following DESIGN.md will fight the implementation.
- **Fix:** Add a hero or mid-page `bg-card` screenshot frame; default marketing to `.dark` on `<html>` or route layout; align display type scale with DESIGN.md.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable craft product-screenshot hero` or `$impeccable layout apps/web`

### [P1] Section eyebrow on every block (AI grammar)

- **What:** Five uppercase tracked labels ("MacOS first desktop UI", "Why it exists", "Workflow", "Status", etc.) in `+page.svelte`.
- **Why it matters:** Impeccable absolute ban; reads as generated landing scaffolding, not a deliberate brand voice.
- **Fix:** Keep at most one kicker for the whole page, or replace with structural cues (spacing, rules, screenshot captions).
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable quieter apps/web`

### [P1] Numbered workflow + side-stripe borders

- **What:** `01 / 02 / 03` steps plus `border-l border-border` on workflow list items (mobile).
- **Why it matters:** Numbered section markers and side-stripe accents are explicit anti-patterns unless order is essential (here it is, but the stripe is not).
- **Fix:** Use a real ordered list without mono step prefixes, or a single horizontal step row; remove `border-l` accent.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout apps/web` or `$impeccable distill apps/web`

### [P2] No product imagery or proof

- **What:** Zero screenshots, video, or UI embeds.
- **Why it matters:** Brand register expects imagery for product landings; solo devs evaluating a desktop tool need to **see** density and transcript chrome.
- **Fix:** Ship at least one credible desktop capture in a card frame; optional second for sidebar/session list.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable craft screenshot section` or `$impeccable bolder apps/web`

### [P2] Dead-end primary CTA

- **What:** Both "Early access soon" buttons are `disabled` with no email capture, GitHub, or docs link.
- **Why it matters:** Jordan (first-timer) and evaluators hit a wall; no peak-end reassurance after reading the page.
- **Fix:** Add one working action: waitlist field, link to GitHub/README, or "View architecture" doc.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify apps/web` or `$impeccable harden apps/web`
